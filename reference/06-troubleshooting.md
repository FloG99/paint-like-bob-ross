# Troubleshooting

> Most problems come from four causes: a wet or dirty brush, too much paint, too much pressure, and too many strokes. Fix while the paint is wet: scrape it off, cover it with a dark, turn it into something, or wipe it back.

Each entry: **what it looks like**, then *cause* and *fix*. Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the point / episodes that paint the subject (or all 379). They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Paint handling
- **Mud**: colors turn grey-brown and the layers churn together.
  - *Cause*: a wet or dirty brush; thick paint laid over thin; too much pressure; working one spot too long. Mud is named as the risk in 21/379, a wet brush in 10/379.
  - *Fix*: wash, shake and beat the brush dry before every blend. Keep base layers firm and dry, and thin the later ones (thin over thick is given as the reason in 120/379). Finish one bush before the next. If it is already mud, scrape it off or cover it with a dark (see Repair moves). [S13E04 06:58, S09E04 07:49, S12E01 15:57, S06E09 13:15]
- **Too thick**: ridges and lumps; the next layer smears instead of sitting on top.
  - *Cause*: too much paint left on the canvas.
  - *Fix*: scrape off the excess with the knife held flat and firm, or pull the 2" down over it to lift paint off. [S12E13 4:58, S08E10 03:51, S07E04 05:38]
- **Too thin or runny**: paint drips; snow sinks into the dark; a highlight vanishes.
  - *Cause*: too much medium, a wet brush, or soft paint. Too much Liquid Clear makes everything slip.
  - *Fix*: firm, dry paint for snow and base layers; a thin, even base coat; very little Liquid Clear. [S07E04 10:07, S12E10 01:50, S11E05 01:16, S09E01 09:30]
- **Paint won't stick**: the highlight skids off, or the brush lifts the paint underneath.
  - *Cause*: the paint on the brush is thicker than the paint on the canvas.
  - *Fix*: dip in Liquid White or a little thinner first, then the color (57/379), and use less pressure. [S31E01 21:07, S07E02 15:53, S31E13 08:18]
- **Overblended**: a flat, lifeless sky; clouds dissolved; no streaks left.
  - *Cause*: too many blending passes.
  - *Fix*: stop early (sky 14/333, clouds 18/201). Leave colors half mixed. To get the life back, add a few fresh light or dark touches and blend once (?). [S07E04 01:17, S07E07 03:45, S11E01 06:03]
- **Lost darks**: the painting looks flat; highlights no longer show.
  - *Cause*: highlights on everything, covering the dark (21/379 warn about this).
  - *Fix*: tap the dark mix back in, then highlight only the edges facing the light. Darks are needed so lights show (62/379). [S11E01 18:13, S02E01 19:18, S06E05 13:25, S17E07 22:16]
- **Too bright, garish**: glaring greens, a sky that looks on fire.
  - *Cause*: pure yellows or crimson in large amounts.
  - *Fix*: dull it: brown in a bright mix, ochre in green, a touch of red in green (79/379 dull a color or keep it from getting too bright). [S06E07 17:51, S04E02 03:19, S31E09 14:26, S03E05 00:54]
- **Unwanted green**: a sky, glow or mountain turns green.
  - *Cause*: blue or black touching yellow; too much blue in a mountain dark.
  - *Fix*: keep yellow away from blue; put crimson or lavender between them; clean the brush in between (34/355 sky, cloud or glow episodes). [S13E04 03:47, S07E02 05:02, S08E06 06:25]

## Sky and clouds
- **Blue streaks**: *cause* color not worked into the brush. *Fix*: tap the brush straight down into the paint until it is even. [S20E09 02:43]
- **Sky too dark or too strong**: *cause* too much of the strong blues. *Fix*: start with very little; it is hard to take off. If the brush picks up too much dark, wash it and go on. [S13E13 00:38, S10E05 05:42]
- **An even sky, nowhere for clouds**: *fix* leave open holes while criss-crossing (44/333). [S05E01 01:17]
- **Dirty glow**: the bright center goes grey or green. *Cause*: a dirty brush dragged into it. *Fix*: start in the light area with a clean brush and work outward (20/347). [S06E07 00:55, S10E08 01:52, S18E04 02:07]
- **Shapeless puff**: *cause* the brush stopped moving. *Fix*: tiny circles, brush always moving; thick, thin and bare in places. [S12E02 3:35]
- **Cloud looks like rain**: *cause* straight-up pulls on the top. *Fix*: lift up and around in circles (7/201). [S11E01 06:54, S10E05 07:39]
- **Cloud gone**: *cause* the top was blended, or the base worked too long. *Fix*: blend the base only (61/201); repaint the body with fresh white. [S11E01 06:15, S15E13 05:55]

## Mountains
- **Snow is mud or looks like frosting**: *cause* pressure, or soft and wet white. *Fix*: firm dry white, a thin roll, no pressure at all (63/192), so the paint breaks. [S10E12 11:07, S05E08 03:48, S12E02 8:41]
- **Snow won't sit on the dark**: *cause* excess dark not scraped. *Fix*: scrape after the outline (52/192), pull down, then float. [S12E13 4:58, S07E04 05:38]
- **Green mountain**: *cause* too much blue in the dark. *Fix*: more crimson and black; it should look black. [S08E06 06:25]
- **Flat peak**: *cause* no shadows. *Fix*: an opposing float beside every highlight (88/192). [S13E02 12:07, S01E06 07:59]
- **Strokes look wrong**: *cause* random angles, or shadows at the highlight angle. *Fix*: one angle per face, along the slope; shadows at the opposite angle. [S19E13 15:03, S12E02 9:20]
- **Ridge too straight**: *fix* break it with a little white and a shadow. [S04E09 12:08, S26E06 07:08]
- **Hard bottom edge**: *cause* sweeping across, or a dry canvas. *Fix*: tap and lift along the slope angles with a clean dry 2" (99/183). [S20E09 12:46, S06E09 06:16]
- **Far range too busy**: *fix* blend it sideways, at most a token highlight (keeping detail low is stated in 52/309 mountain or far-tree episodes). [S20E01 09:08, S19E13 05:49]

## Water
- **Hard edge in the water**: *cause* starting in the middle. *Fix*: pull from the edges inward (42/233); soften a harsh line with short lifts. [S08E05 03:03, S31E01 02:31, S10E12 18:04]
- **No sheen**: *fix* keep a light channel open in the center (35/233); blend across gently so it stays. [S12E02 1:48, S10E06 02:35]
- **Water tilts or runs off**: *cause* crooked lines. *Fix*: every stroke and water line dead level (level or straight in 33/233 water and 28/189 water-line episodes). [S04E02 23:25, S03E02 04:26]
- **Crooked or mirror reflection**: *fix* pull straight down from the object (121/184), then one or two light passes across (128/184); never repaint it. [S20E01 14:46, S09E07 16:55, S12E02 14:06]
- **Land looks level with the water**: *fix* raise the bank, cut a level water line under it, and keep water in the low ground. [S11E01 19:32, S06E12 10:03]
- **White turns to mud in a stream or fall**: *cause* white as thick as the paint under it. *Fix*: dip in Liquid White first so it is thinner. [S08E06 17:37, S14E02 22:05]
- **Too many ripples**: *fix* a few short ones under the line, no more. [S02E08 12:27]

## Trees and bushes
- **Ragged tree, splayed bristles**: *cause* too little paint (lots of paint named in 79/172). *Fix*: reload full for every tree; reshape the chisel edge. [S12E13 17:23, S09E05 20:01]
- **Trees like fence posts**: *cause* even spacing, same height. *Fix*: vary heights, fill in between, cross them, add a smaller companion (the fence look or uneven growth named in 9/300 evergreen and far-tree episodes). [S19E13 17:56, S10E01 16:23, S27E07 14:56]
- **Smeared bush**: *cause* the brush slid. *Fix*: press, bend the bristles up, lift (10/285). [S12E02 18:15]
- **Bush doesn't show**: *cause* no dark behind it. *Fix*: lay or keep a dark mass behind it first (61/285). [S12E13 24:35, S07E04 18:35]
- **Highlights everywhere**: *fix* only the lit edges, strongest at the top, fewer lower; some trees get none. [S13E02 22:59, S10E01 25:29]
- **Random leaves**: *fix* tap clumps limb by limb. [S07E04 25:45]
- **Trunks like poles**: *fix* vary them, bend them, cross limbs over the trunk. [S08E12 08:15, S14E11 24:19, S09E08 20:02]
- **Far trees too sharp**: *fix* tap down with a blue-grey mix, no detail. Too much white at their base keeps blending until it is absorbed. [S12E13 12:59, S31E07 06:24, S12E13 15:27]
- **Green in a winter scene**: *fix* leave sap green out of winter trees. [S15E04 19:04]

## Land and rocks
- **Smeared land**: *cause* dragging. *Fix*: tap straight down (84/264 say tap). [S11E01 18:15, S08E06 18:26]
- **All planes the same**: *fix* each plane forward a little lighter or yellower, with a new stroke angle (45/264). [S15E08 16:00, S11E01 24:22]
- **Grass leans the wrong way**: *fix* follow the lay of the land (86/227). [S23E03 12:09, S11E11 06:28]
- **Grass glares**: *fix* dull it with ochre or sienna + white. [S02E11 12:19, S17E08 14:06]
- **Rock floats or looks stuck on**: *fix* pull some of its color down under it and seat it in land or water. [S02E06 23:23, S22E02 25:40]
- **Rocks lit all over**: *fix* a little light on the top planes only (65/158), same side as the mountain light; keep them dark. [S02E12 11:36, S20E06 23:02]
- **Water or trees with nothing to hold them**: *fix* stones to contain the water, land under trees at the shore. [S25E06 13:31, S09E13 16:42, S20E07 14:12]

## Structures
- **Layers slide on the building**: *cause* the shape was not cut out first. *Fix*: scrape the shape down to the base (82/100). [S06E10 10:20, S10E11 20:26]
- **Cabin looks sunk or crooked**: *fix* roof front higher than the back; check every angle. [S19E04 21:22, S17E08 03:10]
- **Building floats**: *fix* cut its bottom straight, then snow, grass or bushes at the foot (74/100). [S31E12 13:24]
- **Flat or too bright walls**: *fix* leave the mix marbled; keep the first highlight dull. [S27E11 13:45, S03E04 11:17, S15E03 18:02]

## Repair moves
Remarks about repairing or hiding something occur in 20/379.
1. **Scrape off with the knife**: knife flat, firm, take the wrong or heavy paint off; repaint thinner. A whole canvas can be scraped and reused. [S11E13 00:40, S08E10 03:51, S01E06 02:08]
2. **Cover with a dark**: lay the tree or bush dark over the spot; trees and bushes hide weak areas. Water can be laid generously and the extra covered by land later. [S08E06 24:25, S09E05 18:25, S11E07 07:03, S10E03 02:36]
3. **Turn it into a tree or bush**: dark flecks lifted straight up become tiny trees; a blot near the shore can be covered by a bush pushed up over it, then highlighted (?). In the water, pull it straight down into a reflection. [S19E13 16:07, S12E02 21:14, S17E06 12:14, S23E04 20:12]
4. **Wipe back to the base**: rub or wipe with a clean brush or paper towel to lift paint back toward the base; on a gesso ground, wipe with thinner and repaint. [S16E12 19:21, S28E08 22:39, S26E07 17:12]
5. **Blend it out**: a cloud you dislike can be blended away; an edge you dislike pulled straight down. [S24E11 05:23, S30E11 14:28]

## What to check before the next step
- **Base coat**: thin, even and wet; no pools. [S01E01 02:07]
- **Sky**: darkest at the top, light at the horizon, holes left, no streaks.
- **Clouds**: bases soft, tops fluffed, bodies still bumpy.
- **Water base**: pulled from the edges, strokes level, sheen channel open.
- **Mountain dark**: excess scraped, top crisp, base fading.
- **Snow**: paint broken, not smeared; one angle; a shadow beside each highlight; light on the chosen side.
- **Mist**: soft base, no hard line, peaks still sharp.
- **Far trees and shore**: lighter and bluer than what comes later; reflections straight down; water line level.
- **Middle and near darks**: masses dark and dense; land higher than water; brush washed before light colors.
- **Highlights**: darks still visible; lights on one side only; each plane forward lighter and warmer.
- **Finish**: lightest light and darkest dark near the focal point; framing dark at an edge; no detail far away.
