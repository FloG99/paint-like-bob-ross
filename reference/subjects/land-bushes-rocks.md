# Land, grass, bushes, rocks, paths, fences, flowers, desert

> Land is dark tapped down first, then light tapped on layer after layer, each plane a little lighter. Bushes are pushed up, never slid. Rocks are knife slabs, dark, with a little light on top.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Land and grass
1. **Dark land**:
   - Use the 2" flat, tapped straight down (never dragged), in the tree dark with more blue, black, brown or green. Tool counts: knife 109/264, 2" 100/264, fan 89/264; tapping is stated in 84/264, straight down in 19/264, dark in 136/264.
   - Make irregular masses with ragged lower edges (43/264). The land must sit higher than water (water is mentioned in 74/264).
   - Darks are needed so the lights show (31/264).
   - [S11E01 18:15, S08E06 18:26, S30E12 10:29]
2. **Grass**:
   - Use the 2" (88/227) or a fan (108/227), in cadmium yellow (94/227) + sap green (123/227) + yellow ochre (119/227) + Indian yellow (68/227), mixed on the brush (122/227 mention loading or mixing). The load runs out as you tap, which varies the color.
   - Gentle taps down on top of the dark (tap, dab or touch 166/227), in rounded mounds following the lay of the land (86/227), with lit upper edges and dark below (58/227).
   - Start at the far band and come forward; each new plane is a little lighter or yellower (layers or planes 43/227; far-to-front words 86/227).
   - The more you tap, the softer it gets (29/227).
   - [S11E01 22:36, 23:43, S19E13 22:48, S08E04 23:16]
3. **New plane = new angle**: change the stroke angle to start a new plane (45/264 land steps mention angle, plane or layer). Mist goes between layers (named in 4/319 land or grass episodes). [S15E08 16:00, 13:05, S11E01 24:22]
4. **Hill highlights**:
   - Use a dirty 2" with yellow + ochre.
   - Short pressing strokes along the hill contour, lightest on the top and the sun side, darker as the hill turns away (highlight is named in 98/319 land or grass episodes).
   - Make each hill stand out from the one behind it.
   - [S15E08 8:30, S03E10 10:29]
5. **Sparkle**: now and then a touch of bright red in the grass (54/227 have it on the brush). [S11E01 24:10, S19E13 19:41]
6. **Paths**: see Paths below.

## Bushes
- **Push up**:
  - Use the 1" (115/285) pulled through the paint in one direction (a lot of paint), or the 2" (120/285) or a fan (83/285), the corner in 91/285. A round is used in 35/285 and a knife in 24/285.
  - Press, bend the bristles upward, lift (72/285). Do not let the brush slide; push it and bend it (sliding or dragging warned against in 10/285).
  - [S12E02 12:50, S19E13 25:27, S12E02 18:15]
- **Order**: dark mass first, then highlight clumps (yellow-greens, ochre; orange with bright red) on top and on the lit side. One bush at a time, far to near, in layers (89/285). [S07E04 18:35, S08E06 24:11]
  - Dark behind or under (57/285), highlights named (117/285). Paints: yellow 183/285, sap green 153/285, ochre 147/285, bright red 98/285, Liquid White/Clear 51/285.
- **Placement**: at the foot of trees, against darks (they need dark behind them to show) (61/285), on banks and over the edges of rocks (43/285). [S12E13 25:13, S20E09 23:50]
- **Reflections**: stamp straight down under bushes at the water (10/285). [S02E08 10:11, S07E02 20:22]
- **Path framing**: bushes on the sides push a path back (6/285). [S20E09 26:41, S28E01 21:22]
- **Flowers on bushes**: see Flowers below.

## Rocks and stones
1. **Dark**: Van Dyke on the knife (knife 76/158, small knife 19/158; Van Dyke on the load 109/158). Set the edge down and push sideways into flat slabs, stacked, all tilted the same way. Pull a little down at the water for the reflection (18/158). [S07E04 19:55, S08E06 23:34]
   - Slab or stack named in 1/158; following the angles in 17/158. [S17E07 15:18, S07E04 19:55]
2. **Light**: brown + white (or dark sienna + Van Dyke + white) (45/158) with the knife edge on the top planes and the lit edges (65/158). Keep it on the same side as the mountain light. [S08E06 17:19, S17E07 19:08]
3. **Big rock faces**: lights go on by skipping and bouncing the knife (11/158); blend with a dry brush along the rock's direction (16/158). Keep them dark. [S17E07 10:04, S17E07 22:59]
4. **In the water**: a dark blob, its top lit, and water wrapping around it. With a white splash in front (named in 4/158). A rock in front of something pushes it back (19/158). [S19E13 21:28, S10E10 11:51]
5. **Twigs**: scratch them with the clean knife tip through the wet bush paint. These steps are filed under trunk/limbs (89/315, see trees.md), not under bushes (2/285). [S08E06 26:04, S07E04 16:17]

## Flowers
Painted in 8 of 379 episodes, near the very end (mean position 0.84, `05-order.md`). Two kinds.
- **Red dots on bushes**: tiny dots of bright red (6/8) tapped with the tip of a 1" (5/8) on the tips of bushes or grass (6/8). Use few. [S29E01 21:54, S14E01 23:06, S28E12 22:16]
- **Daisies** (2/8; one studied episode, S11E03, on a Liquid Clear base; x, y as canvas fractions) [S11E03 23:00, S27E06 23:03]:
  1. **Mound**: fan, Van Dyke + dark sienna thinned with Liquid Clear, punched in hard so the bristles bend up, then blades pulled up with the corner, curving; ochre blades lighter away from the dark core. One crescent hump at the lower center-left (x 0.1–0.68, y 0.68–0.86). [S11E03 20:40, 22:15]
  2. **White petals**: no. 6 filbert, white dipped in Liquid Clear until quite thin, rewetted as it thickens. One very short press-and-flick per petal, outward from a shared center, about five or six (?) in a loose ring; no outline; heads may tilt. Head about 0.04–0.05 of the width (?). [S11E03 23:00, 23:55]
  3. **Placement**: along the mound's crest and just in front, uneven clusters, never rows (x 0.3–0.65, y 0.58–0.78). [S11E03 23:36]
  4. **Blue daisies**: same filbert wiped, thin phthalo, fewer and lower, among the white. [S11E03 24:24]
  5. **Centers**: one touch of yellow ochre + Clear (~0.01 of the width, ?), not on every flower (centers 3/8). [S11E03 25:50, S27E06 23:29]
  6. **Stems**: liner, brown + thinner, short upward strokes under some flowers so they do not look dropped on. [S11E03 26:10, 26:31]

## Desert
Cactus in 3 episodes (S08E10, S21E11, S25E12). Frame-checked in one studied episode (S21E11: an oval on a portrait canvas, Liquid White; x, y as fractions of the oval). Back-lit; the sky is the warm base of `sky-clouds.md`.
1. **Far butte**: fan corner, lavender with a touch more blue than the cloud lavender, pressed on and pulled **down** in short strokes, each a vertical face, so the top is a row of little spires; a thin flat foot line out to each side; the bottom pulled into haze with the 2". Small, no detail: x 0.22–0.40, summit y 0.52–0.56, on a horizon just below the oval's middle. [S21E11 07:58, 09:18, 03:18] A knife can lay it instead. [S25E12 04:30]
2. **Brown**: sap green + alizarin crimson about 1:1, mixed thoroughly, unlike most mixes; it does ground, rock and cactus. [S21E11 10:24, 10:43]
3. **Ground**: 2", a little brown, very light below the horizon, more paint toward the bottom, so it darkens as it comes forward; the middle stays lightest, the rim darker. [S21E11 10:59, 11:45]
4. **Big rock**: knife, a roll of the brown; block a tall shape and fill it (x 0.58–1.0, y 0.38–0.75, cut off by the oval edge). Then bright red + a little brown + white, **zero pressure**, grazed steeply down the face so the paint breaks into holes; lighter on the left and top faces (?); leave one dark recess. A mountain is treated as a big rock. [S21E11 12:17, 13:42, 14:14, 15:15]
5. **Flat ledge**: paint only the top, then pull the knife down so a little top color drags into a sharp edge. [S21E11 15:51]
6. **Seat it**: pull the rock base out to the left as a dark shadow. A worn path curves from behind the rock to the lower left, widening; blend the light dirt back by pulling one way, then one very light pass across. [S21E11 17:53, 18:22, 19:06]
7. **Dry bush and stones**: a fan pushed upward, then liner branches in thinned brown [S21E11 19:26, 20:05]; stones are one dab each of a filbert double-loaded with Liquid White + brown on one side, brown on the other [S21E11 21:14, 22:02].
8. **Cactus**: knife with the brown; the trunk first with a round top (never square), then one arm each side, the small knife for curves. Ribs: a tiny roll of white + black + brown touching only the edge; a touch of sap green on one side; mostly a silhouette. A soil mound and light flecks at the foot so it does not hang, then liner grass. In S21E11 it stands just outside the oval at the left (x ~0.10, y 0.45–0.93 of the canvas). [S21E11 22:50, 23:16, 24:02, 25:33]

## Mistakes
- Dragging the brush (that gives smears, not texture).
- Bushes with no dark behind them.
- Rocks at random angles.
- Every plane the same value.
