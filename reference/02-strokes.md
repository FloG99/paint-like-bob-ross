# Stroke vocabulary

Every painting is built from about 20 named strokes. Path and angle are given in canvas terms
(x right, y down). Pressure words are defined in `01-materials.md`.

| stroke | tool, part | path and angle | pressure | for | seen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **criss-cross** | 2" flat face | short X strokes ~2–4", alternating ±45°, bouncing; row by row from the top down | light–medium | sky color, any big soft area | S11E01 01:21 |
| **horizontal pull** | 2" flat | long side-to-side strokes across the width; for water, from each edge in toward the middle | medium | water base, sunset bands, sky blend | S12E02 1:48 |
| **dry blend** | clean dry 2" | very light long horizontals or X's over wet color, light area toward dark | whisper–light | sky, water, glow | S12E13 2:47 |
| **tiny circles** | 1" corner (or fan) | small scrubbing circles, ~0.5–1" bumps, brush always moving | medium | cumulus bodies | S12E02 3:35 |
| **base blend** | clean dry brush, corner | tiny circles along the cloud bottom only, never the top | light | cloud undersides | S11E01 06:15 |
| **fluff** | clean dry 2" corner | touch and lift up and around in small circles, never straight up | whisper | cloud tops | S11E01 07:04 |
| **knife outline** | knife long edge, roll | drag along the ridge, lift and re-place for peaks; blade nearly upright; inside doesn't matter | hard | mountain silhouette | S12E02 6:00 |
| **scrape** | knife flat | firm strokes over the shape to take off all excess paint | hard | after every knife outline | S12E13 4:58 |
| **pull-down** | 2" flat | from the top edge straight down or down the slope, lifting off toward the base; then horizontals at the base | firm → light | mountain body and mist base | S11E01 09:13 |
| **knife float** | knife edge, roll | set on the ridge and let it slide down the slope at the slope angle, 0.5–3", only the roll touches; paint breaks | whisper | snow and highlights | S12E13 6:14 |
| **opposing float** | knife edge, roll | same as the float, at the opposite angle, placed beside each highlight on the shadow side | whisper | snow shadows | S12E02 9:20 |
| **tap and lift** | clean dry 2" | tap along the base following the slope angle, lift slightly up on each tap; never sweep across | light | mist at a mountain base, softening | S20E09 12:46 |
| **corner zigzag** | 1" chisel corner or fan corner | thin vertical leader line, then short side-to-side swings down the tree, wider and pressing harder toward the bottom, same corner all the way | light → firm | evergreens | S12E02 16:56 |
| **push up** | fan flat or 1" | press, bend the bristles upward, lift; rows from the top, each wider | firm | evergreens (upward limbs), bushes, splashes | S20E09 17:36 |
| **tap down** | fan edge vertical | short vertical taps, never dragged; each dab is a little spiky tree | light | distant tree lines | S19E13 17:10 |
| **stamp / pounce** | 2" or 1" | push the bristles straight in, wiggle, lift; never slide | firm | dark banks, foliage masses, grass mounds | S08E06 18:26 |
| **lift up** | 1" flat | touch and pull straight up 0.3–0.5", side by side | light | turning dark flecks into tiny distant trees, trunks | S19E13 16:07 |
| **reflection** | 2" flat | from the base of the object pull straight down; then one or two very light horizontals across | light then whisper | every reflection | S12E02 13:54 |
| **water line** | knife long edge, roll of Liquid White | one dead-level line where land meets water, pressed into the fabric; a few short ripples under it | firm (line), light (ripples) | shorelines, the foot of every bank | S12E02 16:00 |
| **cut / scratch** | knife edge or tip, clean or thin roll | thin lines cut through wet dark paint, the canvas shows | light | trunks, twigs, sticks | S11E01 20:05 |
| **knife slab** | knife flat, Van Dyke roll | set the edge down and push sideways in short flat pieces, all tilted the same way | medium | rocks, stones, banks | S07E04 19:45 |
| **skip and bounce** | knife edge, tiny roll | barely touch and lift repeatedly along the rock's angle | whisper | rock highlights | S17E07 10:04 |
| **sheet pull** | 1" or fan flat, Liquid Clear/White + white | touch at the lip, pull straight down, bristles bend; strokes side by side | light | waterfall sheets | S17E07 11:44 |
| **liner pull** | liner, thinned paint | wiggle out from the trunk, lifting off | light | limbs, twigs | S15E08 23:19 |

## Composite gestures
- **Dark, then light**: lay a mass dark with a stamp or zigzag, then give it a few highlight taps with less pressure on the side facing the light. That makes trees, bushes, hills and rocks.
- **Lay, scrape, pull, float, mist**: the whole mountain. See `subjects/mountains.md`.
- **Stamp, then stamp straight down**: while the highlight color is still on the brush, stamp once straight down into the water under each bush. The reflection comes for free. [S12E02 14:20]
