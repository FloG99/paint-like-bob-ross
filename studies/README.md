# Studied episodes

Sixteen episodes studied in depth. These notes are the main evidence behind the reference cards.

Each file contains:
- the order of the steps;
- every step with its tool, load, motion, placement, result and timestamps;
- the rules stated in the episode, paraphrased;
- an analysis of the finished painting.

The narration is read first. Video frames are then checked wherever the words leave something
open, such as angle, direction, size or placement. Inferences are marked `(?)`. Positions are
fractions of the canvas (0 = top or left), accurate to about ±0.05.

**Selection:** the first ten are regular episodes (no guests, no special frames) with mountains,
rivers, streams or waterfalls, spread over seasons 7–20. Water and mountains were chosen because
angles, positions and pressure matter most there. That is a choice of focus, not a judgment of
importance. The last six (seasons 1–21) were added to cover subjects whose cards rested only on
narration counts: ocean, structure (barn), night with northern lights, moon, flowers on a Liquid
Clear base, and desert in an oval. For most of these subjects it is the only frame-checked
evidence, so those parts of the cards rest on one episode (structures also draw on S07E04).

Transcripts and video are not included, for copyright reasons.
