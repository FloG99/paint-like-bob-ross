# Sky and clouds

> Little color tapped into the wet base, criss-crossed: dark at the top, light at the horizon. Clouds are white dropped into holes, with only their bottoms blended.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Sky
1. **Color**:
   - Load the 2" by tapping it into phthalo blue (+ midnight black). For warmth, lay alizarin or Indian yellow + bright red first and the blue over it, with no wash in between.
   - Paint criss-cross X strokes (216/333), row by row from the top, light–medium pressure.
   - The color thins itself in the wet white, so the top corners are darkest.
   - Leave holes: open gaps where clouds go (44/333).
   - Warm color goes in before the blue in 87 of the 125 skies that use both. [S11E01 01:21, S12E13 0:23, S20E09 01:09]
2. **Horizon**: blend downward. The sky must be lighter toward the horizon, and a pale band stays just above the land (48/333). [S12E02 1:22]
3. **Dry blend**: with a washed, dry 2", use very light horizontals or X's, working from the light area toward the dark (71/333). Stop early, because over-blending makes the sky flat and lifeless (14/333). [S07E04 01:47, S12E13 2:47, S10E12 04:31]

## Cumulus
1. **Body**:
   - Use the 1" brush (or the fan, or the 2" corner), pulled through white with a touch of bright red. Load a lot (55/201).
   - Paint tiny circles, or dance and jiggle, so the top is bumpy and the base flat (85/201 describe circles). Keep the brush moving or it becomes a heavy, shapeless puff.
   - Make it thick in places, thin in others, and bare in some (26/201).
   - [S12E02 3:35, S07E04 02:27]
2. **Base**: with a clean dry brush corner, make tiny circles on the bottom only (61/201 blend the base). Never touch the top (21/201 say so). The more you work it, the more the cloud disappears (18/201). [S11E01 06:15, S10E05 06:59]
3. **Top**: fluff it (90/201). Barely touch and lift up and around in circles, never straight up (a feather-light touch). Straight pulls read as rain (7/201). [S11E01 07:04, S10E05 07:39]
4. **Shading variant**: a blue or lavender band can go in first and the white be built on its upper edge, or lavender can go under the white (50/201 lay blue with no white). The light comes from above. [S15E08 1:30, S19E13 00:30, S20E09 04:45]
5. **Layers**: paint the farthest cloud first, then clouds in front of clouds (58/201). A small cloud tucked in front adds depth. [S19E13 04:12, S20E09 06:54]

## Glow and sunset
- **Warm color first**:
  - Lay Indian yellow, then crimson over it, in horizontal bands with one unwashed brush. Then add blue at the top.
  - Blue and black go *around* the glow, never over it.
  - Use tiny amounts, best on Liquid Clear.
  - Usually no sun disc, only the glow (a disc appears in 19/69 sun/glow episodes: a finger-tapped white spot, lifted with the knife or left soft [S10E12 06:32, S30E12 04:55]).
  - [S12E10 00:30, S08E06 00:24]
- **Glow from a bright center**: crisscross yellow or white X's around the spot (33/69), blend outward with a big brush, and keep the dirty brush off the center, so it fades darker toward the edges (25/69). [S03E10 00:26, S01E05 00:55]
- **Streak clouds**: rub white on with a flat knife edge, horizontally, short strokes, then blend lightly (knife in 12/201 cloud episodes). A fan, floating and stringy near the horizon, is the more common tool (38/201). [S12E10 05:00, S08E10 03:12]
- **Water**: whatever goes into the sky goes into the water too, a little brighter where the sky is brighter. [S12E10 02:13]

## Variants across episodes
- **Prussian instead of phthalo**, or blue + black: Prussian, black or both in 124/333 skies. Phthalo is still the common blue (193/333). [S01E01 04:12, S31E12 00:29]
- **Lavender or pink sky**: crimson X's first, then blue over it, blend carefully because the blue is much stronger (68/333; 14/333 state the warning). [S29E01 01:21, 02:12, S31E13 02:35]
- **Warm base**: Indian yellow, ochre or cadmium yellow before anything else, then crimson or red above it (100/333 skies use a yellow). [S31E13 00:38, S12E10 00:00]
- **White in the sky mix**: white + a touch of crimson, then phthalo for lavender, with a 1" around the sun (85/333 use white). [S10E12 00:40, 01:56]
- **Dark or dull clouds**: blue + brown, or black, tapped with the fan (25/201). [S02E04 02:52, S10E05 03:43]
- **Cloud tools**: 2" corner or flat 149/201, fan 74/201 (mostly the corner, 35/201), 1" 40/201. [S20E09 04:41, S12E02 03:32]
- **Stringy clouds**: thin wisps with a fan, low near the horizon (25/201). [S08E10 07:45]

## Night and moon
Only 3 episodes paint a moon as a step (S01E06, S03E02, S03E08), all early seasons; the word moon appears in 5 (also S19E12, S08E10). Treat this as a thin sample. Night skies start from a dark base, so light is added, not dark (see `01-materials.md`). Bullets headed *S01E06* are frame-checked in that one studied episode (x, y as canvas fractions).
1. **Light first**: little white X's around the moon spot, circling outward and fading, with the sky kept dark so it still reads as night. [S01E06 00:26, 00:53, S03E02 00:22, S03E08 02:35]
   - *S01E06*: a 1–2" brush (?), straight white, short X's spiraling out from the moon spot, light even pressure; the white picks up the dark ground and grays at the rim. Halo near (0.5, 0.25) (?), 0.25–0.3 of the width across, plus a soft band low across the sky (y ~0.6–0.7, ?), the glow bounced up off the horizon. Knit it in with very gentle X's of a large dry brush; corners stay black. [S01E06 00:26, 00:47, 01:21]
2. **Moon**: push white in and turn it, or finger-paint a small circle. Scrape the excess with a knife, light touch, then blend back and forth or crosswise with the big brush until it settles into the sky. Go gently, since several layers are down. [S01E06 01:45, 02:08, S03E02 01:09, S03E08 03:20]
   - *S01E06*: a small brush full of white, held end-on, pressed straight in hard with one twist at the halo's center; the flat knife slides off the excess at the rim; a clean large brush passes very lightly. Disc 0.04–0.06 of the width, halo about five times that. [S01E06 01:47, 02:02, 02:25] A finger-painted circle also appears on a Clear-over-black sky. [S19E12 04:09]
3. **Clouds**: fan corner, one passing across the moon, small ones high and low. A little crimson in the sky changes the shade of a second cloud. [S01E06 02:38, 04:53, S03E02 01:57, S03E08 04:02, 05:17]
   - *S01E06* dark first: fan corner, a lot of Prussian blue + Van Dyke, short strokes pushed hard along a horizontal path: flat bottoms, ragged tops. One long cloud just under the moon's center, a chunkier one above right, thin ones low. [S01E06 02:39, 03:25]
   - *S01E06* light: clean dry fan corner, white + a little of the dark, pressed along the edge facing the moon: the top edge on clouds level with or below it, the underside on a cloud above it. Then horizontal sweeps of a dry large brush, barely touching. [S01E06 03:56, 04:54, 05:22]
4. **Finish**: blend the clouds darker away from the moon. [S03E02 03:11]
5. **Below a night sky** (both night studies): the usual knife mountain with very little paint, the ground being dark already [S08E13 04:44]; trees left near-black, a few blue taps at most [S01E06 27:25, S08E13 23:34]; snow bushes of white + Liquid White pushed onto dark shapes [S08E13 21:57, S01E06 10:18]; reflections of white alone pulled straight down, then lightly across [S08E13 14:52, S01E06 11:08]. Neither paints the moon or aurora in the water.

## Aurora (northern lights)
Named in 2 episodes (S08E13, S29E12), both on a black base under transparent color. Frame-checked in one studied episode (S08E13), where it is painted first.
1. **Curtains**: a fan flat to the canvas, full of white only (the color comes from the ground); tap, never drag, along a curve, building a thin wavering stippled band about 0.02 wide, with more paint where it should be brighter. S08E13 has three nested curtains: from the left edge (y ~0.2) arcing to (0.55, 0.5); from the top (x ~0.2) down to (0.6, 0.5); the biggest from the top right out to (0.9, 0.25) and hooking back to (0.5, 0.5). [S08E13 01:32, 01:44, S29E12 01:12]
2. **Short rays**: a 1" flat, nothing added, set on the band and flicked upward in a short pop all along the arc, each ray following the curve's local direction, 0.1–0.25 of the height. The lower edge stays hard and bright; the rays fade upward, picking up the ground colors. [S08E13 02:22, 04:00]
3. **Long rays**: the 2" flat, long upward lifts through every band at the same angles, as high as wanted. Lift only, never scrub. Leave the inside of the curve dark. [S08E13 03:11, 03:25]
4. Where the lights are not seen, a small moon instead. [S08E13 01:11]

## Mistakes
- An even, flat sky with no holes.
- Clouds blended on top, or worked too long.
- Wet brushes.
- Too much paint, especially of the strong blues.
