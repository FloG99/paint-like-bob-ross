# Water: lakes, reflections, water lines

> The water goes in with the sky brush, before any land. Reflections are the wet paint above, pulled straight down and then lightly across. A dead-level knife line of Liquid White separates land from water.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Lake
1. **Base**:
   - Use the same dirty 2" as the sky (phthalo + black; add phthalo green for teal). (2" 129/233 episodes with water, fan 62/233, knife 45/233; blue paint 153/233, green 47/233, dark 58/233; same or dirty brush as the sky 41/233)
   - Horizontal pulls (121/233), starting from the left and right edges toward the middle and from the bottom up, so the color thins toward the horizon (17/233 say the direction).
   - Leave an unpainted light channel in the center (narrow at the horizon, widening toward the viewer) as the sheen. (35/233)
   - Start from the outside and pull inward. No strong edges.
   - [S12E02 1:48, S07E04 00:52]
2. **Blend**: very light horizontal passes with the dry brush. The sheen stays. (36/233 name a blend step)
3. Everything on the shore is painted **over** this base later.

## Reflections
1. As soon as a shore, tree line or tree is painted, put the 2" flat at its base and **pull straight down** about the height of the object (0.08–0.2 of the canvas). Use one pull per clump, light pressure, and no new paint: it is the same wet paint. [S12E02 13:54, 21:05]
   - Straight down in 121/184 episodes with reflection; 2"/1" in 114/184, fan 43/184, knife 17/184; light or whisper pressure 157/184; "no new paint / clean brush" stated in 22/184 [S08E13 14:33, S02E04 19:08].
   - One pull per clump named in 5/184 [S02E04 20:01]; some episodes tap or dab instead (42/184) [S01E02 15:50, S03E04 20:27].
2. Then go **lightly across** once or twice (just enough to distort it). (128/184) [S12E02 14:06, S12E10 14:01, S07E04 16:00]
3. Highlights reflect too (6/184): while the highlight color is still on the brush, stamp straight down under each bush, then soften with a hair-light pull down and across. [S12E02 14:20, 15:38, S07E04 19:15]
4. Anything that went wrong can be turned into a reflection. Never crooked: always straight down. [S12E02 21:14]

## Water lines
1. **Load**: Liquid White pulled flat on the palette, a thin roll cut on the long edge. Add a touch of crimson to warm it, or use dark sienna + white for a far shore. [S12E02 16:00, S20E09 16:41]
   - Knife in 139/189 episodes with a water line; white paint 152/189; Liquid White or Liquid Clear 104/189; a warm tint (red/sienna/brown) 53/189; a roll or long edge named 25/189; a fan, liner or brush instead 42/189. [S12E10 15:58, S07E04 16:00]
2. **Main line**: one perfectly straight horizontal line exactly where land meets its reflection (straight or level named in 40/189). Press it in, then let it wander off at the end (3/189 [S13E07 14:15]).
3. **Ripples**: a few short horizontal ripples below. Keep them few (ripple named in 9/189 [S06E04 16:33, S02E08 12:27]).
4. They must be **straight and level**, or the water appears to run off (10/189 give this reason [S04E02 23:25, S03E02 04:26]). They also cut off the bottoms of the land masses cleanly (92/189 describe the cut as clean or crisp). [S12E02 16:41, S11E01 26:49]
5. Repeat under every bank and island (64/189 name a bank, island, tree or base). [S12E02 25:55, S20E09 21:57]

## Shore and land edge
- The land must sit **higher** than the water. Water normally sits in a recessed area (40/269 say lower, recessed or higher). [S11E01 19:32, S12E02 25:44, S12E02 26:02]
- **Bank wedge**:
  1. With a Van Dyke roll on the knife, lay a thin tapering wedge along each bank at water level, thick at the canvas side, the tip pointing into the water.
  2. A slight pull gives the lay of the land.
  3. Add brown + white stones tilted the same way along its top.
  4. Cut a white water line under it.

  [S12E02 25:17, 25:55]
- **Snow lip** (winter): a thin white ribbon along the shore, bumpy and slanted, with a dead-level water line under it. (3/280) [S20E09 21:56, S19E01 15:46]

## Variants across episodes
- **Water laid in with the sky** and blended with it (15/233 mention the sky as the place or reason): a blue band at the bottom of the sky with the 2", pulled from the outside in [S12E06 01:59], or sky and water blended together lightly [S17E08 02:33].
- **Fan instead of the sky brush** for the base (62/233): [S11E01 26:12, S15E08 19:59].
- **Knife water** (45/233): small puddles pulled down with Prussian blue or white for a sheen, then a light pass across [S01E01 17:15, 17:42], or a pale rub around a stone [S03E02 15:25].
- **Sparkles or highlights** on the water, sparingly (15/233): [S01E01 19:00, S09E02 11:32, S09E12 19:12].
- **Light streak under the moon**: a knife pushes light down under it (3/184 reflections). [S03E02 04:55]

## Mistakes
- Water painted after the land.
- Reflections that are crooked, too long, or repainted as a mirror image.
- Wavy water lines.
- Too many ripples.
- Land at the same height as the water.
