# Trees

> Every tree is dark first, with a lot of paint. Light goes on the side facing the light, sparingly. Far trees are tiny, blue-grey and simple; near ones are big, near-black and framing. Never a fence: vary heights and group them.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Evergreens
1. **Load**:
   - Use a fan loaded full on both sides (tool is a fan in 142/172) or a 1" brush pulled and wiggled to a chisel edge (sharpening 18/172; 1" 15/172).
   - Mix: the mountain dark (black + Prussian + Van Dyke + crimson). Add sap green in summer (81/172); none in winter.
   - Use lots of paint, or the bristles will not hold together (79/172). Reload for every tree (8/172).
   - [S12E02 16:56, S12E13 17:23, S11E01 13:51]
2. **Leader**: one thin vertical line with the corner from the top down (tops first, 35/172). [S11E01 14:46, S10E01 16:23]
3. **Branches**, in one of three styles:
   - *Corner zigzag*: short side-to-side swings down the tree, wider and with more pressure as you go, so more of the brush touches. Keep the same corner all the way down (56/172). [S12E02 16:31, S12E13 18:13]
   - *Push up*: rows from the top, each pressed and bent upward, wider toward the bottom (the strokes go upward) (36/172). [S08E06 20:33, S20E09 17:36]
   - *Tap*: plain taps with a loaded fan (76/172; 27/172 say straight down). [S11E01 14:46, S10E01 15:44]
4. **Groups**: a big tree with a smaller companion tree (44/172). Fill in the back of the tree to merge trees into masses. Use few branches where the mountain should show through (16/172 place them over or against the mountain), and leave sky holes (9/172). [S12E13 19:30, S13E10 14:35, S17E07 20:34]
5. **Trunk**: a short pull down at the base, or a knife cut of dark sienna + white through the paint. Not in every tree (trunk named in 10/172). [S11E01 19:51]
6. **Highlight** (named in 93/172):
   - Use the dirty brush + cadmium yellow (dark green) on the lit edges of the limb clusters (39/172). [S29E01 11:46, S20E01 20:18]
   - In winter use phthalo + white (frost) (blue-white highlight named in 38/172; winter or snow named in 7/172). [S12E13 26:30, S08E13 10:46]
   - Make them strongest at the top and fewer lower down (15/172). Some trees get none.
   - Evergreens are quite dark. Do not kill all the dark.
   - [S11E01 21:36, S20E09 24:51, S19E13 25:18]

## Distant tree lines
- **Fan tap down**:
  - Hold the fan edge vertical and use short vertical taps, never dragged. Each dab is a spiky tree. Tools: fan 100/255, 2" 94/255, round 35/255, 1" 34/255, liner 17/255. Tap or touch 197/255; down 41/255; vertical edge 17/255.
  - Use the dark + white (blue-grey, 83/255 have white on the brush) or lavender (36/255). The farther away, the lighter (31/255).
  - Make them dense, or they look like a fence (named in 3/255). Make a few taller spires (named in 0/255).
  - [S19E13 17:10, S12E13 12:40, S15E08 10:14]
- **Layers**: one band of trees behind the next, each layer darker or closer than the last (91/255; darker layer in front of a lighter one 27/255). [S15E08 13:27, S29E01 05:32]
- **Trunks and mist**: touch the base with a little white and lift straight up. This gives pale trunk hints and a misty bottom (lift or pull up in 45/255; trunks named in 20/255). [S12E13 15:05, S19E13 18:42, S29E01 08:48]
- **Knife conifers**: firm vertical knife strokes in clusters (12/255). [S15E08 11:22, S29E01 06:42]
- **Base**: a horizontal strip along the root line (22/255 name a base or strip), then reflections and a water line under it. [S19E13 15:33]

## Leaf trees (deciduous)
1. **Dark mass**:
   - Use the 2" (116/275) or a fan (116/275) or the 1" (65/275), tapped or pushed (96/275 say push or press), in Prussian blue + Van Dyke + crimson (+ sap green, on the brush in 149/275).
   - Make the mass lighter toward the top (90/275 mention top or lighter). The big tree often runs off the canvas (named in 8/275).
   - [S15E08 22:16, S12E10 16:42, S15E08 22:31]
2. **Trunks and limbs**: see the next section. Order: back of the tree first (43/315 mention back or behind), then trunk, then highlights. [S12E10 17:40, S15E08 23:19, S07E04 23:35]
3. **Leaves**:
   - Use the 1" or 2" corner (corner in 107/275), a touch of Liquid White/Clear (39/275), then yellow + the dark (green) (yellow on the brush in 168/275), then yellow ochre (121/275), then a touch of bright red (73/275).
   - Tap clumps limb by limb (24/275). Think about individual limbs rather than throwing them on at random. [S06E04 07:03, S10E13 10:14]
   - Make it airy and loose, with a lot of paint, and let the dark show between clumps (31/275). [S15E08 24:25, S07E04 25:06, S17E07 20:26]
4. **Autumn sheets**: big orange areas can be laid with tilted horizontal sweeping strokes sloping down, the grey-blue under them showing between the layers. Sweeping named in 2/275. [S15E08 25:10, S13E04 19:15]
5. **Thousands of leaves**: the 2" corner dipped in clear, ochre + bright red, many tiny taps (27/275). [S12E10 26:06, S08E06 18:01]

## Trunks and limbs
- **Tools**: a fan brush or knife for trunks (Van Dyke, or dark sienna + white); knife 175/315, fan 109/315, the 1"/2" 42/315; a liner for limbs (205/315). Paints: Van Dyke (204/315), white (208/315), dark sienna (113/315). [S12E10 18:29, S29E01 23:10]
- **Limbs**: a liner with thinned paint or Liquid Black (144/315 say thinner; Liquid Black 14/315), wiggled out from the trunk. [S07E04 23:12, S12E10 24:56]
- **Sticks and twigs**: scrape the clean knife edge or tip through the wet paint of bushes or dark masses (89/315). Light-colored twigs on dark and dark twigs on light both occur. [S11E01 19:51, S20E09 20:27, S29E01 23:36]
- **Highlight**: light on the side facing the light, barely touched and pulled (110/315). [S12E10 24:15, S07E04 24:29]
- **Birch**: use knife pulls around the trunk (birch named in 22/315). [S31E13 22:05, S15E04 24:31]

## Mistakes
- Trees all the same height, or evenly spaced.
- Highlighting everything.
- Green in winter trees.
- Too little paint, so the bristles splay.
- Far trees with detail.
- Leaves thrown on at random.
