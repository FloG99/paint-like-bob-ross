# S15E08 Fall Stream
- palette: phthalo blue, alizarin crimson, midnight black, Van Dyke brown, Prussian blue, dark sienna, cadmium yellow, Indian yellow, yellow ochre, bright red, titanium white (+ liquid white as thinner/base)
- canvas: thin even coat of Liquid White, wet. Nothing pre-painted. 4:3 landscape.
- light: sun high, behind/upper left (clouds glow pink, hill tops lit yellow-green, dark side of trunks to the right); clearing fall day. Cool lavender distance, warm yellow/orange middle and right, green foreground.

## Steps
### 0:21 Sky: phthalo blue
- tool: 2in brush, flat bristle face on canvas
- load: phthalo blue worked a small amount into the bristles (small load, no white added; canvas already wet white)
- motion: little criss-cross X strokes ~2-3 in, light-medium pressure, along top edge then down; color thins itself in the liquid white; reload a little and continue. Then blend the whole sky with a feather-light touch (brush barely touching)
- placement: solid blue across top; fades by ~y0.5 to bare white-grey; blue also comes lower at left edge (~y0.35-0.4, x0-0.1)
- result: deep blue top graded to near white at horizon
- rule: the tone brightens progressively as it nears the far line [0:43-0:55]
- seen: 0:46, 1:12, 1:25

### 1:30 Cloud shadow base
- tool: 2in brush (same, unwashed)
- load: phthalo + alizarin crimson, just a slight lavender tint
- motion: small round circular strokes, light scrub; dark band built from overlapping circles, left to right
- placement: upper left (x0-0.4, y~0.25-0.3), band continues right sloping down to ~y0.4 at x0.9 (?)
- result: lumpy dark blue-purple band with stippled edge (3:20 frame)
- rule: dark first, with the lights added on a return pass [1:53]
- seen: 1:52, 2:14, 3:20

### 2:13 Brush change
- wash 2in in odorless thinner, shake off, beat it out vigorously; take 1in brush, pull through white; least touch of alizarin

### 2:45 Cloud tops
- tool: 1in brush, flat face
- load: titanium white (stiff) + least touch alizarin; extra touch later for warm pink
- motion: small circular strokes laid on the TOP edge of each dark base, billow after billow along the band, ragged edges; then gently blend where dark meets white, beating brush between passes; finally fluff the tops with a very light circular touch
- placement: whole band, white above, dark underneath; pink in cloud centres
- result: big cumulus mass, white tops, lavender-blue underside; reaches top-left corner and runs right to ~x0.8
- rule: paint that is quite dry and stiff, or it turns to mud [3:30-4:45]
- seen: 2:58, 3:30, 4:06, 4:24, 4:42

### 5:00 Distant mountains
- tool: knife, then 2in brush
- load: alizarin >> phthalo (far more crimson) = dark lavender, a roll of paint on the knife edge
- motion: knife edge pushed firmly into the canvas, strokes rounded on top; peaks/rounded humps; then 2in brush, very gently lifting that paint, pulling it downward and blending the base into the wet white
- placement: range spans full width, highest peak ~x0.45, y~0.37; flanks ~y0.43-0.5; base fades by ~y0.55
- result: soft lavender mountains with pale mist base
- rule: softening the bottom gives the look of mist sinking toward the base [6:22]
- seen: 5:22, 5:56, 6:13, 6:30

### 6:31 Rolling hills (dark)
- tool: 2in brush
- load: same lavender + black, tapped in
- motion: rounded hill outline pressed with bristle tips, then dabs pulled down vertically = stippled fuzzy mass beneath each rounded top; second lower layer
- placement: first band tops y~0.47-0.52, second y~0.57-0.6 with tallest hump right of center (~x0.55)
- result: dark purple stippled hills (7:17 frame)
- seen: 6:56, 7:17

### 7:29 Dark fill
- tool: 2in brush
- load: Van Dyke, black, crimson, touch blue, plenty
- motion: scrub across horizontally over the whole lower area (the way a roller works)
- placement: from ~y0.6 to bottom, edge to edge; upper edge soft against hills
- result: near-black purple-brown underpaint; trees, stream and land are all worked into/over it
- rule: scene good for liquid black [8:01]; this spares a great deal of effort afterwards [8:22]
- seen: 7:59, 8:20

### 8:30 Hill highlights (yellow-green)
- tool: same dirty 2in brush (still carries the dark), color tapped into bristles
- load: cad yellow + touch yellow ochre -> olive-green on top of dark; titanium white added where a hill must stand out; touch bright red on one hill
- motion: short pressing strokes following the hill contour with bristle edge, tapped, mottled; paint lightest/thickest on top and sun side, darker as the hill turns away; repeated layer after layer
- placement: left hill first (x0-0.3, y~0.5-0.6), then center hill (x0.4-0.65, peak y~0.5), then right/front hill (x0.5-0.85 down to ~y0.63); dark lavender left showing between hills
- result: lit yellow-green hill tops dropping into purple shadow
- rule: make the one hill separate visually from the other [9:09]; keep layering repeatedly [9:48]
- seen: 8:52, 9:14, 9:36, 9:58, 10:20

### 10:40 Tree patches on hills
- tool: fan brush, tapped
- load: lavender
- motion: tap small tufts
- placement: in the valley between the two main hills (~x0.55-0.7, y0.55-0.6) and a couple elsewhere; clean edges with the 2in afterwards
- result: dark purple speckled patch
- seen: 10:51, 11:17

### 11:22 Conifers (knife)
- tool: clean knife (wiped on paper towel), edge
- load: blue + black + alizarin crimson, roll on edge
- motion: firm vertical strokes, pressing hard and striking with force; edge pressed, pulled/pushed up-down with a small wiggle so the top is a spire and the base ragged (?); many clusters, varied heights
- placement: starts mid (tall tree ~x0.42), spreads both ways across ~x0.1-0.9, tops y~0.58-0.65 dropping into the dark fill
- result: row of dark blue spruce against the yellow hills
- seen: 12:00, 12:09, 12:15, 12:30

### 12:36 Mist at base of trees
- tool: clean dry 2in brush
- motion: tap firmly with bristle ends at the bottom of the trunks; the aim is to alter the shape without wrecking it
- result: stippled haze under the trees
- rule: a layer of mist is needed between each stage [13:05]
- seen: 12:52, 13:03

### 13:19 Closer conifers
- tool: 2in brush, same dark
- motion: tapped vertically with bristle tips, pointed uneven tops, layer after layer
- placement: in front of and below the first row, tops ~y0.62-0.68, full width
- result: denser darker tree mass, hazy bottoms
- seen: 13:24, 13:35, 13:40

### 13:41 Autumn bushes
- tool: 2in brush, corner only
- load: yellow (cad / Indian) + bright red (+ ochre ?) tapped directly into the corner
- motion: tap the corner, small rounded clumps with slightly pointed tops, overlapping; start at one spot (~x0.35, y0.69) and add across
- placement: foot of the conifers, y~0.67-0.73, partly overlapping trees, x~0.12-0.82, orange/red/gold mix, uneven spacing
- result: orange and gold bush clumps against dark blue trees
- rule: employ only one corner of the brush, which yields many attractive small tree shapes [14:06]
- seen: 14:08, 14:16, 14:28, 14:41, 14:54, 15:07

### 15:24 Mist, then meadow
- tool: clean dry 2in brush; then same brush tapped into yellows and greens
- motion: light tapping to blur base; then tap greens horizontally, shifting the angle to make a new plane, coming down
- placement: meadow band y~0.72-0.8 below the bushes; dark ground still shows lower
- result: soft green meadow
- seen: 15:20, 15:40, 16:20, 16:40

### 17:09 Dirt bank

- load: dark brown, then brown + white
- motion: drag edge left to right in a wavy line; then thin brown+white highlight along top edge as pale pebbles/stones
- placement: from left edge (~y0.80) to ~x0.7, y0.86; water below stays dark
- result: dark wavy bank edge, then pale pebbly strip
- seen: 17:18, 17:26, 17:50, 18:13

### 17:47 Bank grass
- tool: fan brush
- load: little yellow + green
- motion: small upward flicks/taps down into the bank; he notes that rocks and stones tidy up the edges
- seen: 18:01, 18:13

### 18:24 Stream
- tool: a small fine-tipped tool held pencil-like; knife or liner (?)
- load: dip into liquid white then titanium white + a little phthalo; liquid white only thins white so it flows over the dark
- motion: thin curved horizontal pulls following the stream; short broken white dashes for splashes; widens toward viewer
- placement: begins left (~x0.08, y0.86), runs right and widens to x~0.7, y0.95-1.0; the dark ground is the water, only the streaks are added
- result: rippled bright streaks on dark water
- rule: liquid white is only there to loosen the titanium white [18:31]
- seen: 18:53, 19:03, 19:11, 19:20

### 19:31 Stones
- tool: knife
- load: Van Dyke, then brown + white for tops
- motion: press flat, drag sideways to make flat blobs; small highlight on top; set them down in place
- placement: in stream ~x0.5-0.7, y0.9-0.97 and ~x0.3, y0.88; more along bank
- result: flat brown rocks, dark below, pale top
- seen: 19:40, 19:55

### 20:03 Water over stones
- tool: knife
- load: liquid white + titanium white + touch phthalo
- motion: pull blade down over/below each stone in short strokes (ridged falls), then horizontal splashes downstream; another at bottom edge
- result: white falls below each stone, foam in front
- seen: 20:10, 20:20, 20:25, 20:34

### 20:45 Foreground land

- load: lavender/dark, then dark green + yellow; Indian yellow, ochre, bright red touches
- motion: left slope tapped up along a diagonal toward the stream with bristle tips, lighter green at the top edge; right side grass brought down to bank
- placement: left wedge x0-0.55, top edge up to y~0.8 at stream; right side below stones
- result: green slope with stippled edge
- seen: 21:38, 21:48, 21:59

- motion: tapped/pushed vertically along right edge; tree runs off canvas; second leaner hanging over the scene at ~x0.62, y0.3-0.45; third leaner at left edge

## Rules and principles he states
- Sky lighter toward horizon [0:43].
- Paint very dry and firm or it turns to mud [1:30-4:40].
- Wash brush with odorless thinner, beat it [2:17].
- Dark first, then highlight [1:53; 6:55].
- Mist between each layer separates them [13:05].
- Corner of the brush makes tree shapes [14:06].
- Change angle -> new plane [16:00].
- Individual limbs, not random [24:51].
- Leave dark under trees [26:07].
- Liquid white thins white to flow over color [18:31].

## Finished painting
- focal point: center hill and mountain peak ~x0.45, y0.4-0.6; stream lower center (x0.1-0.7, y0.85-1.0) leads in
- planes: far = sky, clouds (upper left), lavender mountains (y0.37-0.5); middle = yellow-green hills (y0.5-0.65), blue conifer row (y0.58-0.75), orange bushes; near = green meadow, stream and stones, left and right trees
- darkest darks: right tree trunks and foliage mass, left edge, conifers
- lightest lights: clouds upper left, stream foam
- framing: right tree (x0.7-1.0) with orange/green leaves, thinner left tree
- proportions: sky ~35%, hills/trees ~35%, foreground ~30%, water ~10%
- light: from upper left/behind; cool top, warm middle, green near

## Surprises
- Whole lower 40% painted dark up front; everything below the hills placed on that.
- Stream improvised late; bank done before water.
- Orange leaves on right tree put on with horizontal sweeping strokes, not small taps.
