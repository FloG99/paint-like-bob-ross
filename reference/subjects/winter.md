# Winter and snow

> Cold palette: blue, black, Prussian, white, plus crimson if you want it warm. Little green, and yellow kept out of the snow itself. Snow is white laid over dark: shapes come from the dark around it and blue shadows under it.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

## Palette
- **Warm winter**: alizarin crimson in the sky, the snow and the lavenders. Some red-family paint is used in 38/46 winter episodes, so warm is the norm.
- **Cold winter**: leave out the pink and use blue, grey and black (no red at all in 5/46). [S20E09 03:30, 03:47, S02E04 20:25, S11E11 10:51]
- **Little green**: green snow looks wrong. Sap green shows up in 7/46 winter episodes (vs 33/101 of all snow episodes, where snow just caps peaks) and across all episodes in 20% (vs 76% of all). [S12E13 21:34, `dataset/stats.md`]
- **Yellow is not gone**: it appears in 30/46 winter episodes, mostly in the sky glow, lit windows and ochre on bushes, but in the snow steps themselves in 4/46 (ochre to break up a hard bank edge). [S11E11 19:56, 21:54]

## Snow on the ground
1. **Banks**:
   - Use the 2" with titanium white (43/101), after grabbing some of the tree dark first. Pull or drag it (66/101).
   - Long, slightly curved horizontal pulls following the ground contour, rising and falling. Form the crest edge first, then fill the face below with smooth strokes parallel to the crest.
   - Make it smooth and untextured: pristine snow.
   - [S12E13 22:50, S31E12 06:40]
2. **Shadows**: drag dark into the white under the trees, then lay white over it (white picks up the surrounding dark in 32/101). There is no separate shadow step. Blue shadow bands lie in the hollows and under the trees (29/101 name a blue shadow). [S12E13 23:13, S25E03 23:45]
3. **Path**: with the fan, zigzag back and forth, the path growing larger toward the viewer. Soft or distinct, as wanted (a snow-on-path step named in 10/101, zigzag in 5/101). [S12E13 23:54]
4. **Lip at water**: a thin white ribbon along the shore with the knife, bumpy and slanted, a dead-level Liquid White water line under it (named in 8/101). [S20E09 21:57, S10E12 25:54]

**Why snow doesn't read as water.** It sits on shore forms:
- bumpy, slanted crest edges;
- broken by dark bushes and trees;
- with blue shadows under the trees.

Water, by contrast, has a hard level line and vertical reflections. [S20E09 notes]

## Snow on bushes and trees
- **Snow bushes**:
  - Dip the 1" in Liquid White (liquid white in the bush steps of 26/101), then load a lot of titanium white (+ crimson for warm).
  - Touch against the dark and push up a little: lacy tufts in clusters of 2–3, on the top and lit side of dark bush masses (snow-covered bush steps in 26/101).
  - It needs the dark behind it so it shows up (29/101 say so).
  - [S12E13 24:54, S20E09 20:49, S10E12 22:35]
- **Frost on evergreens**: a few dabs of phthalo + white on the lit edges, mostly near the top (white or blue-white dabs on trees in 36/101; with blue 32/101). Keep the contrast. [S12E13 26:30, S20E09 24:51, S08E12 25:09]
- **Fine flecks**: a liner or fan corner for specks on branches (liner in 14/101). [S11E11 25:47, S08E03 26:02]

## Snowy mountains
Same as `mountains.md` (35/101 snow steps are on mountains, 23 with the knife):
- **Lit faces**: white, or white + a touch of crimson.
- **Shadow faces**: white + Prussian.
- **Every highlight** gets a shadow beside it.
- **Lower slopes**: broad light white sweeps arcing outward and down. [S12E13 9:40, S08E13 05:45]

## Variants across episodes
- **Roofs and cabins** (33/101; knife 22/101): pull a knife-edge of white down the roof at the roof's angle, then touch the top and the far side. A blue edge goes on the shadow side (19/101 add one). [S08E13 25:21, S11E02 17:12, S19E04 24:08, S08E12 19:56]
- **Color left showing through**: lay a warm or pink base, then tap thick white over it so the pinks and blues stay visible, or use a light touch with the fan so the underlay makes the shadows (15/101). [S11E02 15:16, S08E12 14:57, S10E08 15:58]
- **Cool touch**: a little blue worked into the white on shadow sides (20/101). [S25E03 11:26, S08E12 22:50]
- **Brown pulled in**: white with Van Dyke or sienna for bank shadows, instead of blue (13/101). [S11E11 10:51, S08E12 22:50]
- **Thinner highlights**: Liquid White mixed into the titanium white for a slightly thinner paint on trees and far banks (19/101 use Liquid White). [S10E12 17:27, S13E02 17:54]
- **Knife for the edges**: cut or push the snow edge with the knife to tidy it (21/101 use the knife this way). [S11E11 11:30, 12:09]

## Mistakes
- Green or yellow in the snow.
- White that is too thin, so it disappears into the dark.
- Snow with no dark around it.
- Snow banks laid flat with level edges (they read as water).
