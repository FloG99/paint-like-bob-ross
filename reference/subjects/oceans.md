# Oceans: waves, foam, beach

> A seascape is a dark sea with one big wave sketched in first, then lit from inside: shadow color goes in before the light, a scrubbed yellow-white glow fills the thin part of the wave, and the crest, spray and foam are pushed up from it with the brush edge. Foam is broken so the dark shows through. Beach and wet sand come last and stay low and light under the foam, with rocks to give the crash something to hit. (33 episodes paint waves, 19 paint a beach.)

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said. Bullets headed *S10E10* are frame-checked in that one studied episode (x, y as canvas fractions).

## Steps
1. **Sky and far water**:
   - The sky goes in first (often a warm glow at the horizon), then a straight horizon line.
   - Lay the sea with the 2" in dark phthalo/Prussian blue + crimson, with black and ochre or phthalo green blended in toward the viewer for a green cast; pull across, darker toward the front.
   - Liquid Clear under it keeps the dark true and the light area transparent. (stated in 2/33 wave steps) [S10E10 10:29, S09E12 08:28]
   - *S10E10* split coat: Liquid White on the sky down to the horizon (y ~0.44), Liquid Clear below, since on white the sea dark would weaken. [S10E10 00:14, 09:35]
   - [S09E02 06:24, 08:19, S12E09 07:23, 09:09, S09E12 07:13]
2. **Sketch the wave first**: with a filbert and a mid lavender (blue + crimson) or thin white, mark the big wave line or curl shape and the area to leave clear. (23/33; filbert 21/33). The stated reason is that it avoids trouble later. [S10E10 08:33, S12E09 06:12, S09E12 07:51, S11E10 10:52]
   - *S10E10*: dark thinned to water; crest from the left edge (y ~0.60) arcing to a peak at (0.47, 0.50); a second line loops up into the crash hook at (0.8, 0.48); shoreline at y ~0.75–0.78. [S10E10 08:31]
3. **Block in the dark**: fill around the sketch with dark blue + crimson (or phthalo green, or black + blue) with the 2" or filbert, and put the dark in the curl and behind the crash. Dark before light. (stated 8/33) [S10E10 10:29, 15:49, S12E09 09:57, S09E12 08:28]
   - *S10E10*: 2" flat, thick paint, side-to-side passes wrapping the guide. Two dark bands, far sea (y 0.45–0.55) and near sea (y ~0.63–0.84, bellying up under the wave), with the wave face left bare between. [S10E10 09:30]
   - *S10E10* headlands: fan corner, little dark paint, tap and drag along the right end of the horizon (x ~0.75–1.0, y 0.40–0.50), a lower, lighter shape first, a bigger, darker one in front; outline only. [S10E10 11:22]
4. **Far swells**: with a fan, barely touch and pull small white strokes, short and gentle. Leave dark spots between them. Fade them out toward the horizon. (17/33; fan 31/33) [S10E10 15:10, S09E02 09:36, S09E12 09:39, S03E02 06:39]
   - *S10E10*: two or three stacked ridges, each with a gentle upward bump (x 0–0.55, y ~0.47–0.58), then a clean fan pulls them sideways along their angle. The dark between matters more than the light. [S10E10 13:00, 14:23, 14:41]
   - For tiny far-off waves the knife can be pushed so the blade bends. (knife 12/33) [S12E09 11:13, S24E10 14:06]
5. **Wave body**:
   - Pull white with the fan along the wave's angle, wiping the brush between strokes, then blend back with a clean, nearly dry fan so the trough forms by itself. (16/33) [S11E10 12:47, S12E05 10:12, S12E09 14:33]
   - Keep the dark edge behind the wave. [S11E10 12:47, S12E05 09:00]
   - *S10E10* crash: fan, a dark crescent of blue + crimson along the guide inside the curl (x ~0.55–0.8, y ~0.52–0.66); then heavy white set on the curl's outer edge and pulled inward and down along the arc of the lip, wiping the brush after each stroke (wiping named in 11/33). [S10E10 15:30, 16:17, 16:35]
   - Soften with a clean dry 2" using very light upward strokes. (whisper pressure 20/33) [S09E02 13:29, S10E10 17:45]
   - *S10E10* (after the glow): upward pulls curving with the crest carry lavender from the near band (y ~0.68) up into the yellow (y ~0.5); the crest line stays crisp. [S10E10 17:47]
6. **Glow**: scrub white + a touch of cad yellow into the thin, transparent part of the wave with the filbert (firm, reload often), then work outward from the bright center toward the dark. Yellow over blue turns green, which reads as light through water. (glow/eye 16/33; scrub 17/33; yellow in 27/33) [S10E10 17:06, S09E02 12:11, S12E09 17:38, S02E09 16:54]
   - *S10E10*: white + the least cad yellow, scrubbed firmly along the top of the wave, where the water is thinnest: a triangle, peak at the crest (x ~0.47), base x 0.2–0.7 at y ~0.65. [S10E10 16:59, 17:29]
7. **Crest and curl**:
   - The curl is a C-shaped or upward pull of white from the fan, bending the brush over. (curl/crash/spill 27/33) [S10E05 19:47, S09E02 14:08, S03E02 08:45]
   - Blend it with tiny circles of the 2" top corner. (15/33) [S10E10 21:41, S12E09 18:37]
   - Lay the lavender shadow in first, then the bright edge on top by tilting the brush and pushing, so the bright edge is left. [S09E02 14:49, 16:10, S12E09 19:30, 20:19]
8. **Foam and spray**:
   - Push the brush upward at the end of a stroke, or turn it on its side, for splashes. (26/33; splash 19/33) [S10E05 17:53, S10E10 19:42, S11E10 17:55]
   - Foam is small patterns made with rocking or dabbing strokes of the fan or filbert; leave gaps so the sand and dark water show through. (dab/tap 16/33; gaps stated 5/33) [S12E09 22:31, 23:22, S10E05 24:18, S09E06 20:00]
   - Shadow foam first in lavender + a little white, then the white and yellow on top. [S10E10 18:26, 19:42]
   - *S10E10*: shadow scrubbed rough with the filbert flat, below and inside the crash (x ~0.6–0.85, y ~0.55–0.7). Then a clean filbert filled with white + cad yellow is held on the shadow and given one short upward push per splash, along the lip, tallest at the hook; wipe and turn the brush instead of washing it (upward push named in 12/33). [S10E10 18:37, 19:54, 19:48]
   - *S10E10* near water: fan, white + the least phthalo, short lines along the swell (y ~0.62–0.8) and long curved strokes rising from the lower left toward the crest; dark left between them (stated 4/33). [S10E10 22:56, 24:41, 24:58]
   - Foam is not all bright white: add blue and crimson to the white for the body of the wave. [S09E02 18:09, S09E12 16:02]
9. **Dark line under the wave**: a thin line of dark blue + crimson with the liner (or the knife) along the foot of the wave makes it stand up. (13/33; liner 24/33) [S12E09 24:09, S09E02 24:39, S04E05 25:49, S13E12 19:13]
10. **Sparkles**: thin yellow + white flecks, only under the light and not everywhere. (9/33) [S10E10 22:23, S11E10 24:16, S13E12 21:58]
    - *S10E10*: thinned as thin as ink, tiny filbert dabs down the crest and along the crash. [S10E10 22:09, 22:15]
11. **Beach and wet sand** (19 episodes):
    - Sand is brown/sienna/ochre (12/19) mixed with white (15/19), laid with a fan or knife as a strip below the foam, lighter toward the water. (sand 16/19; fan 9/19, knife 6/19) [S10E10 24:58, S09E02 20:07, S12E09 10:22, S09E12 18:19]
    - Wet sand: pull straight down under the foam with a clean dry brush, then blend outward. (10/19) [S09E02 21:25, S03E02 10:50, S09E06 21:16]
    - Do not start dark here, or it eats the light area. [S09E02 21:25]
    - A thin blue-white line at the edge shows water on the sand. [S10E10 25:35, S12E09 23:21]
    - *S10E10* sunset sand: in the bare Clear strip (y ~0.84–1.0), side-by-side vertical patches of the sky colors (cad yellow, ochre, bright red), sienna + Van Dyke at both ends, blended lightly side to side; dark at the sides makes the middle glow, read as the sky reflected (?). Then a brown + white dry-sand wedge at the lower right (x ~0.45–1.0) and thin light-blue water lines. (yellow/red/ochre in 6/19) [S10E10 23:36, 24:12, 25:23, 26:04]
12. **Rocks**: a dark brown + sienna stone shape at the base of the wave (knife, fan or filbert), a light graze on top with ochre, then splash water up its side. A stone gives the crash something to hit. (rock/stone in 10/33) [S10E05 21:06, S09E12 17:19, S11E10 21:02, S02E09 13:32]
    - *S10E10* stones on wet sand: a Van Dyke dab pulled a little straight down as a reflection, a knife touch of light. [S10E10 26:24]

## Layout (one studied episode: S10E10, landscape, back-lit)
- Sky ~44%, sea ~38%, beach ~18% (?); glow center-left (x ~0.3–0.55, y ~0.1–0.35), purple cloud mass upper right, headlands at the right horizon.
- Focal point: the crash (x ~0.8, y ~0.48) backed by the glowing crest (x ~0.47, y ~0.5), with the darkest darks right behind and under them.

## Variants across episodes
- **Calm bay**: only small ripples and a little foam at the shore. (small waves/ripples 15/33) [S01E09 09:56, S03E02 06:39]
- **Rocky coast on a dark base**: waves of Magic White or Liquid White thrown up against the stones, with blue + yellow added in the wave and a dark line between. [S02E09 13:06, S04E05 17:44, 21:16]
- **Lit wave**: the full glow-and-lavender sequence above. [S10E10, S09E02, S12E09, S12E05]
- **Moonlit or lighthouse seascape**: thin white strokes on a dark sea, blended away from the light, with a wet-sand reflection pulled down. [S03E02 06:39, S03E08 09:18, 23:04]
- **Liner finish**: drips of blue and white down the side of the wave or rock. (3/33) [S10E05 26:14, S04E05 24:57]

## Mistakes
- Light before shadow (shadow is placed first so the highlight shows).
- Foam as solid white with no dark gaps.
- Too many sparkles.
- Dark paint put on the sand first.
- Wavy horizon: a straight line keeps the water from running off.
- Blending with a dirty brush: keep the wave clean.
