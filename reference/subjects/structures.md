# Structures: cabins, barns, mills, bridges

> A building is cut out of the wet land with the knife: scrape the shape, lay the dark mass, rub light across the sunny faces for boards, put a roof on top, then add door, window and snow. Keep it rough. Next to a mountain keep it small so it doesn't compete with the peak; without one it can be the focal point (S07E07). Plant the ground and bushes around its base so it sits in the land. Knife work dominates: the knife is the tool in 91/100 episodes and the first tool in 78/100.

Counts (N/M): episodes whose narration names the step / episodes that paint the subject. They are lower bounds: not everything done is said.

100 episodes paint a structure (barns 18, bridges 7, mills 3, the rest cabins and houses). Bullets headed *S07E07* (a barn built with the 2"), *S08E13*, *S01E06* (night cabins) or *Studied* are frame-checked; x, y are canvas fractions.

## Cabin or barn, in painting order
1. **Cut the shape**: scrape or cut the basic box out of the wet paint with the knife edge, down to the base coat, so the building has a clean pale footprint (82/100). Small, low, and usually set back in the middle ground. [S08E13 24:04, S31E12 07:28, S01E06 23:17]
   - Scraping also clears loose paint so the next layer sticks, and sets the perspective (stated in 42/100). [S07E07 14:57, S01E06 23:03]
   - *S07E07*: knife tip scratches the ridge, both roof edges, the wall corner and a wing to each side through the finished tree line and snow; then the flat blade wipes the area bare. Ridge peak (0.61, 0.43). [S07E07 14:55]
2. **Dark mass**: fill the shape with van Dyke brown, often with dark sienna, as a roll of paint on the knife (brown 89/100, sienna 68/100, edge 74/100). The far eave goes in dark first, then the front. In 46/100 the first structure step is this dark with no white. [S31E12 08:40, S03E04 09:58, S05E13 09:36]
   - *S08E13, S01E06*: Van Dyke (+ dark sienna) pulled across with the knife, far eave first. [S08E13 24:20, S01E06 23:26]
3. **Dark side**: put a darker pass (brown or black) on the shaded face and under the eaves so the lit face reads brighter (52/100). [S31E12 10:49, S08E12 18:42]
4. **Light on the boards**: brown + white on the knife, touched on so the paint barely catches, then cut or bounced downward for old slabs and boards (boards 63/100; barely touch, bounce, graze or whisper 51/100). Light comes from one side only. Keep the first highlight dull; it brightens later. [S07E04 21:15, S08E03 13:26, S31E12 09:54, S03E04 11:17]
   - *S07E07* boards: a Van Dyke roll, knife edge vertical, pulled down for dark seams, unevenly spaced; a clean brush pulls down gently over them. Uneven boards read as old. [S07E07 18:34, 19:09]
5. **Roof**: lay a roll of color along the roof line with the knife (84/100). Red + brown for a shingle roof (red on 30/100 roofs), dark sienna + white or blue/black/white for a weathered or snow roof. Let the paint fall off the knife and pull it down in one stroke. [S07E04 21:54, S10E12 23:19, S07E12 09:05]
6. **Snow on the roof** (winter scenes: 30/43 structure episodes that have snow): white on the knife edge, outline the roof and pull down with a slight overhang. A touch of blue + white on the shaded roof side. [S08E13 25:21, S31E12 11:43, S08E12 19:21, S01E06 23:41]
   - *Studied*: one plane at a time, each stroke following that plane's slope, a row per stroke, ending straight at the eave; leave a thin dark edge along the rake. [S07E07 19:56, S08E13 25:14, S01E06 23:41]
7. **Door**: brown with a white outline or highlight around it, drawn with the knife (door 69/100). [S08E13 24:41, S07E12 12:24, S02E04 22:44]
   - *S07E07*: a dark rectangle under the peak, a third of the wall wide, standing on the snow; top corners cut on a slant with the small knife; a thin light-brown outline makes it stand out. [S07E07 19:36, 20:50, 21:12]
8. **Windows** (43/100): a small blue + white or dark patch with the small knife, or a dab of yellow for a lit window, outlined in black with the liner tip. [S08E03 14:40, S03E04 15:08, S08E12 22:19]
   - Neither studied night cabin has a window: it reads as a dark mass under a bright roof, the only warm color in the picture. [S08E13 24:08, S01E06 23:00]
9. **Extras**: a lean-to shed against the side (29/100, dark wall first, then its own roof) [S05E13 11:43, S01E06 25:18, S08E12 21:10]; a chimney, a flick of bright red or gray from the knife edge (8/100) [S08E03 14:40, S03E04 17:44]; icicles hung with the liner under snowy eaves [S08E12 20:34].
10. **Seat it** (74/100 have the base planted within a few steps): snow, grass or bushes right at the foot, the building's bottom cut straight across with the knife first. [S31E12 13:24] Snow around the base is the most common (38), then grass (32) and yellow-green bushes (26). Push the bush up against the wall. [S31E12 14:09, S07E04 21:54, S01E06 26:14, S02E04 23:46]
   - *Studied*: warm bushes (red + white + ochre) beside a snowy barn keep the scene from feeling cold [S07E07 22:47, 23:23]; at night, snow bushes with knife-scratched twigs beside the cabin and a broken snow slope from its base down to the lower right [S08E13 25:20, 26:28, S01E06 26:22].
11. **Last**: path or fence near it (path after the building in 30/100, fence in 19/100); big trees in front are added last and may overlap the house. [S05E13 14:07, S08E03 15:16, S03E04 19:51]

## Variants across episodes
- **Barn**: same sequence, bigger; one large door plus an old shed added on; shingle indications in red along the roof; grass around the foot (18/100). [S05E13 11:16, S31E12 07:28]
- **Bridge** (7/100): dark brown span laid with the 1" brush over the water behind bushes, small posts with the knife tip, a rail with a white highlight, then pushed back behind the bushes. [S10E04 13:49, 14:17, 14:41, 15:49]
- **Mill and wheel** (3/100) and **steps or porch** (9/100) follow the same knife method. [S03E09 07:15, S03E01 20:09]
- **Built with the liner**: log lines pulled in layers, back eave first, instead of knife slabs. [S03E04 09:58, 11:17] (the liner is the first tool in 1/100)
- **Built with the 2" chisel edge** (14/100 use chisel, 2" or dry brush): the big old building filled in with the brush, wood highlights with a shaking dry brush barely touching, then the knife for boards and roof. [S07E12 05:12, 07:08, 11:43, S08E12 16:31]
  - *S07E07*: (1) 2" full of Van Dyke on both sides, wiggled to a sharp chisel. (2) The rake first: one dark diagonal about 25–30° below horizontal, (0.55, 0.43) to (0.82, 0.57). (3) Wall: strokes pulled straight down from the rake to y ~0.82, then a low wing down at each side. (4) Wipe, load white + dark sienna, drag down over the dark with no pressure so the paint skips into grain. [S07E07 16:12, 17:10, 17:30, 17:44]

## Placement
Small, low and off to one side (x 0.12–0.22, y 0.72–0.82 in S07E04), on the shore or in the middle ground. With a mountain it is a secondary interest that never competes with the focal peak (night cabins at the lower left: x 0.12–0.28, y 0.72–0.88 in S08E13). Without one, the building can be the focal point: the S07E07 barn is large and central (gable x 0.43–0.80, y 0.43–0.82), its snowy roof against the glow. In the order, structure comes at a mean position 0.50 of the painting, with the land and trees; fence (0.79) and path (0.74) come later. In the studies it comes at 0.55 (after trees and snow) and 0.82–0.9 (night cabins, after the bushes). [S07E07 14:55, S08E13 24:08, S01E06 23:00]
