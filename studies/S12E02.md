- palette: titanium white (Liquid White), phthalo blue, midnight black, bright red, Prussian blue, Van Dyke brown, alizarin crimson, sap green, cad yellow, yellow ochre, Indian yellow, dark sienna
- canvas: 18x24, Liquid White coat (wet), nothing pre-painted
- light: sun high from the right (?) (snow lit on right-facing slopes, shadows on left faces); clear cold day, bright haze/sheen low over the center channel of water, a few pink-tinged cumulus

## Steps
### 0:26 Sky
- tool: 2-in brush (the 2-in size he returns to later; size not said here) (?), bristles flat to canvas
- load: phthalo blue (a touch, very strong) + midnight black, tapped into the bristles; add more as needed
- motion: small X / criss-cross strokes (~2-3 in) from the top edge, left to right, row by row downward; color mixes into the Liquid White and thins by itself; then blend and very light long horizontal strokes (~full width) to remove brush marks
- placement: dark at top edge, fades to nearly white at about 0.45-0.55 of height (horizon); lower half stays white for the water/glow
- result: deep blue top, pale hazy horizon band
- rule: the sky should get lighter toward the horizon and stay darker at the top and bottom [1:22]
- seen: 0:53, 1:07, 1:34

### 1:48 Water base
- tool: same dirty 2-in brush, flat
- load: same blue + black, re-tapped (add some more of the color [2:26])
- motion: horizontal strokes starting at the canvas edge (left or right) and pulled inward toward the center; start at the bottom, work upward so the color thins toward the horizon; left side and right side separately; finish with the brush barely grazing the canvas horizontally across everything
- placement: water fills the lower ~0.45; leave an unpainted light channel in the middle, narrow at the horizon, widening toward the bottom (V / hourglass), about 0.3 of width at the middle (?); reads as a sheen
- result: blue edges left and right, white-blue center sheen, soft edges
- rule: begin at the outside edges and pull toward the middle [2:06]; there should be no hard edges [2:21]
- seen: 2:15, 2:42, 2:56

### 3:35 Clouds
- tool: 1-in brush (washed in thinner and beaten dry first [3:05]); fan or 2-in also possible
- load: titanium white + touch of bright red (ends up faintly pink), load a good amount of color into the bristles
- motion: keep the brush moving in tight little circular strokes, building the top as a cluster of rounded humps
- placement: upper-left bank (x 0-0.4, y 0.05-0.28), larger upper-right bank (x 0.4-1.0, y 0.02-0.32), smaller third cloud (x ~0.5-0.7, y ~0.3) (?)
- result: bright puffy lumps with flat blue sky between
- seen: 4:00, 4:12, 4:24, 4:49

### 4:58 Cloud bases and fluff
- tool: clean DRY 1-in brush
- motion: tiny circles along the bottom of each cloud to blend the base out into the sky, then very light lift upward to fluff, then blend again
- result: soft base, loose fluffy top
- rule: make sure the brush is thoroughly dry [5:01]
- seen: 5:13, 5:25, 5:37

### 6:00 Mountain body
- tool: palette knife, long edge
- load: black + Prussian blue + Van Dyke + alizarin crimson, a dark mix; a small roll of paint right on the edge of the knife (paint pulled flat on palette, knife cut across it)
- motion: push very firmly with the blade standing nearly upright to the canvas; draw the SILHOUETTE: from the peak push down-left in a long low slope with ragged end, and from the peak down-right; second lower peak on the right; inside is a rough fill; scrape the excess off and scrape back over the shape firmly (canvas is tough)
- placement: main peak x~0.32-0.40, y~0.12-0.15; left foot x~0.1; right slope to x~0.75; second peak x~0.5-0.55, y~0.28; base around y 0.45-0.5 over the light horizon area
- result: black wedge mountain with ragged base ends
- rule: the only concern is the outer edge [6:32]
- seen: 6:21, 6:34, 6:38, 6:46

### 7:16 Pull base into mist
- tool: large (2-in) brush, flat
- motion: pull the dark down from the base of the mountain and blend sideways with horizontal strokes so the base dissolves; the top edge stays sharp
- result: mountain sharp on top, fading to haze at the bottom, floats
- rule: the top should be sharper than the bottom because of mist [7:26]
- seen: 7:20, 7:32, 7:44

### 8:10 Snow highlights
- tool: knife, tip/point
- load: titanium white + just a touch of bright red (make the white blaze), firm dry paint, small roll on the edge
- motion: tip placed on the peak, ZERO pressure, floated downward along the slope so the paint breaks and leaves holes (dark shows through); strokes follow the ridge angle; all highlight angles run the same direction (down-right on the right-hand slope); ~2-4 in strokes, worked back and forth to redefine firm edges
- placement: right-facing side of each ridge from each peak downward; left side stays dark
- result: pink-white broken snow caps
- rule: make sure the paint is firm and quite dry [8:54]
- seen: 8:33, 8:43, 9:02, 9:12

### 9:20 Snow shadows
- tool: knife
- load: white + a little Prussian blue (very little), pulled out flat, roll on the edge
- motion: same light touch but angles opposite to the highlights (down-left); placed against the highlights on the shadow side; each peak gets its own shadow; to push a peak back put its shadow in first and let the front peak's highlight come through on top
- result: pale blue planes beside pink snow; peaks pop
- rule: every highlight should get a shadow of its own [12:09]
- seen: 9:21, 9:50, 10:00, 10:17, 10:31

### 10:45 Mist at base
- tool: clean dry 2-in brush
- motion: follow the slope angles of the mountain, very light taps, lift upward (barely any pressure), only along the base
- seen: 10:53, 11:00, 11:08

### 11:17 Front range
- tool: knife, same pale mixes
- motion: a pink-white highlight laid as a low bump in front of the main mountain, then slip back in with blue shadow beneath it; a couple more; then dry brush mist along the new base, horizontal wipes
- placement: left-center in front of the main mountain, x~0.2-0.55, y~0.38-0.52
- result: lower snow-field wedge in front of the main peak
- seen: 11:30, 11:44, 12:06, 12:35, 12:42

### 12:50 Far shore bushes
- tool: 1-in brush, bristles tapped into the dark mountain mix + a little sap green
- motion: push the brush upward and bend it (tips stamp, never slide); short clumps chained left to right
- placement: horizontal band centered, x~0.25-0.72, y~0.53-0.58; small at the ends, taller clumps in the middle and right
- result: ragged dark tree line sitting on the water
- rule: press the brush in rather than dragging it, and bend the bristles [18:39]
- seen: 13:30, 13:39, 13:55

### 13:54 First reflection (far shore)
- tool: wide brush (2-in) flat, vertical stroke (?)
- load: the wet dark paint already on the canvas, nothing new
- motion: place at the base of the bushes and pull STRAIGHT down, ~0.08-0.1 of canvas height, one pull per clump; then lightly go across horizontally (light wiping pass) just enough to distort/soften
- result: dark vertical streaks under the tree line, softened with horizontal haze
- rule: pull straight down, then go lightly across, only enough to distort the image [14:06]
- seen: 14:04, 14:07, 14:10, 14:13

### 14:20 Far shore highlights and their reflections
- tool: 1-in brush
- load: touch of liquid white, then through yellow (sap), ochre, Indian yellow, a little bright red; plenty of paint
- motion: tap clumps on top of the dark tree line, push-up stamps, vary colors left to right (greens middle/right, orange/ochre left); farthest first; while the paint is still on the brush stamp it straight down into the water under each clump
- placement: same band; tallest green clump at about x 0.55-0.62
- result: green-orange bushes over a dark base, bright smears below
- seen: 14:41, 14:49, 14:56, 15:10, 15:18, 15:32

### 15:38 Highlight reflections smoothed
- tool: 2-in brush, flat
- motion: very light pull straight down through the bright smears, then very lightly across (almost no pressure; a gentle touch)
- result: soft mirror image of the shore, colors blurred downward
- seen: 15:47, 15:54

### 16:00 Water line (far)
- tool: knife
- load: dark sienna + white + a little brown, pulled flat, small roll on the edge
- motion: firm pressure, blade edge flat and horizontal to the canvas, one perfectly straight horizontal line right where the bushes meet the reflection, pressed firmly into the canvas; then a few short horizontal ripple dashes under it
- placement: y~0.62, x~0.25-0.75
- result: crisp thin beige-white line separating land and reflection
- rule: water lines and ripples should run nearly level [16:41]
- seen: 16:20, 16:24, 16:28, 16:36

### 16:56 Left evergreens
- tool: 1-in brush, corner (chisel) edge
- load: very dark mix (black, Prussian, Van Dyke, crimson, sap), brush pulled through the pile and wiggled to make a sharp chisel edge, a lot of paint; reshape the chisel between each tree
- motion: first pull a thin vertical line (the leader); then with the corner zigzag back and forth downward (short horizontal swings ~0.5 in at the top to ~2 in at the bottom); more pressure toward the bottom so the bristles bend and the tree widens; keep the brush angle the same all the way down
- placement: tree 1 at x~0.12-0.17, top y~0.25 (?), base at the waterline (y~0.62); tree 2 beside it on the right; small third at the far-left edge; trees overlap the mountain's left foot
- result: layered dark triangles with ragged tiers, wider at the base
- rule: add more and more pressure until the brush bends [17:52]
- seen: 17:28, 17:35, 17:41, 18:00, 18:10, 18:20

### 18:20 Left under-bushes
- tool: same 1-in
- motion: push the brush upward under the trees (stamp, not slide), a few dabs
- seen: 18:30

### 18:50 Left tree reflection
- tool: 1-in brush (?)
- load: the same dark
- motion: from the tree and bush bases pull straight down ~0.2 of height with ragged taps; lighten with a horizontal pass (?)
- result: dark ragged reflection under the left trees
- seen: 18:40, 18:50, 19:02

### 19:15 Right evergreens
- tool: 1-in brush corner
- load: same dark, reloaded to a chisel edge
- motion: tall thin leader line, then zigzag-shaped trees: corner swings left-right-left, widening, pressure increasing; a second shorter tree to its left; then more dark bush mass dabbed around and below by pushing upward
- placement: big tree leader at x~0.88, top y~0.03 near the top edge, base x~0.8-0.95; second tree x~0.75-0.8, top y~0.22; dark mass fills x~0.68-1.0, y~0.4-0.72, tapering into the shore
- seen: 19:26, 19:39, 19:51, 20:03, 20:15, 20:28, 20:52

### 21:05 Right reflection
- tool: 2-in brush, flat, vertical strokes down to the canvas bottom edge
- motion: at the line where land and water meet, grab the dark and pull STRAIGHT down, strokes side by side, then a light horizontal pass; ends ~0.2 of height below the shore
- result: dark triangular reflection under the right mass
- rule: pull straight down; reflections should not look crooked [21:14]; anything you dislike can be turned into reflections [21:29]
- seen: 21:13, 21:21, 21:29, 21:37

### 21:50 Trunks and twigs
- tool: knife edge
- load: dark sienna + white, pulled flat, thin roll
- motion: thin vertical slivers pressed into the dark foliage, partly hidden; scraping lines through the foliage for twigs
- seen: 22:02, 24:20

### 22:30 Tree highlights (both groups)
- tool: 1-in brush
- load: yellow + a little black (dark green), brush pulled in one direction; later yellow ochre; bright red on top here and there
- motion: stamp upward clumps along the limbs (think the way a real tree grows), back (far) to front; each highlight is followed by a stamp straight down into the water; if paint won't stick add a little thinner or liquid white
- seen: 22:54, 23:03, 23:22, 23:41, 23:51

### 24:07 Bank bushes and trees

- motion: layered clumps at the shorelines of both banks, push upward, furthest first; orange/rust clump on the left bank at x~0.05; bushes overlap the water edge
- placement: right bank bushes x~0.55-1.0, y~0.45-0.72; left bank x~0.02-0.45, y~0.55-0.77

### 25:03 Bank reflections

### 25:17 Land

- rule: water normally sits in a sunken area [25:44]

### 25:55 Rocks and bank water lines

- load: dark sienna + Van Dyke + a little white; then pure liquid white on a clean flat edge
- motion: short angled dabs along the top of the land strip, blocks all tilted the same way (the angles matter a great deal), reading as stones; then white water lines cut straight and horizontal under the bank, a few short ripples
- seen: 25:57, 26:01, 26:12, 26:16, 26:24

## Rules and principles he states
- sky lighter at the horizon, darker at top and bottom [1:22]
- water: start from the outside, pull in, bottom to top, no hard edges [2:06]; leave a light area open as a sheen [2:35]
- clean brush: odorless thinner, shake, beat it [3:08]; clean DRY brush for cloud bases and mist [5:01]
- snow paint must be firm and dry or it turns to mud; no pressure [8:41]
- shadows run opposite angle to highlights [9:44]; each highlight needs a shadow [12:09]
- mountain sharper on top, mist at the base [7:26]
- work far to near, back layer first, for depth [23:21]
- reflections: pull straight down, then lightly across; hair-light pressure [14:06, 21:14, 25:05]
- water lines and ripples must be straight or the water seems to flow away [16:41]
- bushes: push, bend, never slide [18:39]
- evergreens: sharp chisel edge between trees, same angle, more pressure lower [17:43]
- land must settle the trees; water sits lower [25:15, 25:44]
- bright red: only a touch in white [8:10]; Prussian blue is very strong [9:28]

## Finished painting
- focal point: main snow peak at ~(0.32, 0.13), with the river channel leading to it; bright water at ~(0.5, 0.85)
- far plane: sky (dark top, pale horizon) with clouds upper left/right; mountain range x 0.1-0.78, y 0.12-0.5, pink-white snow on the right slopes, blue shadows on the left; low front snow field x 0.25-0.6, y 0.38-0.52; mist band at y~0.5
- middle plane: long thin far shore (green/orange bushes over a dark base) x 0.22-0.75, y 0.53-0.63, with mirror reflection and a thin straight water line
- near plane: left dark evergreens (x 0.02-0.25, top y 0.15) with orange/green bushes and a rocky bank at y~0.77 pointing right; right tall evergreen x~0.88 (top y 0.03) with bright green bushes and rock bank at y~0.75 pointing left; bright blue water in the channel between
- darkest darks: evergreens and their reflections; lightest lights: snow and cloud tops, water sheen at the center
- proportions: sky ~0.35 (clouds in the upper half), mountains ~0.15-0.2, water ~0.4
- temperature: cool blues in sky, water and shadows; warm accents in the orange/rust bushes and pink in snow and clouds

## Surprises
- The reflection is not painted as a mirror image: the wet dark that made the trees is pulled straight down, highlights are added afterward and pulled down too.
- The mountain is a hard dark silhouette with the knife; snow is a zero-pressure floating knife tip; the misty base is made by dragging the dark down with the brush.
- Water lines are cut with a knife (dark sienna + white), straight and horizontal, not with a brush.
- The center of the water is left unpainted white from the first step and becomes the sheen the bank reflections sit in.
